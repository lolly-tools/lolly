# Lolly 1.1.3: the readable one

These notes cover the 133 pull requests merged to `main` after [v1.1.2](https://github.com/lolly-tools/lolly/releases/tag/v1.1.2) up to `b6db266a1` (29 October 2026), plus this release's own preparation. The engine moves from 1.281.0 to 1.285.0. The tool-author core stays at 1.5.0.

1.1.3 is the first release where everything you install carries the same number: the apps, the web image and archive, the CLI and terminal app on npm, Lolly Work, its Helm chart, the YunoHost packages, the ready-made package and the appliance image are all 1.1.3. The SDK (`@lolly-tools/core`) and the engine keep their own version numbers, because they describe the tool-author interface rather than something people install.

Lolly 1.1.3 makes things easier to read. The Settings **Design system** tab reads like a magazine of what your design system holds, and a strip of covers switches between systems. lolly.tools now offers Lolly Starter beside Lolly Demo, and either can be edited, reset or removed. The `#/start` surface has one name, **Design System Setup**, and its Tokens room is written for someone who has never seen a design token. A design system can carry brand skills, its own written standards for agents, and Design's **Before you export** card reports house rules and a writing signal while you work, without blocking anything. Large Design documents pan, zoom, edit and undo with far less work per frame. Projects can create text and Markdown files and open them in the new Write utility. URL Screenshot's SVGs are geometry instead of a picture. The documentation was rewritten for readers and corrected in 553 places.

## Design systems

- **Lolly Starter beside Lolly Demo.** On lolly.tools, everyone now starts with two design systems: Lolly Demo and Lolly Starter. Lolly Starter is an ordinary local system that you can switch to, edit or remove. A removed companion is not added again on that browser. A catalog lists its companions in `assets/index.json` as `companionDesignSystems`; a locked build gets none.
- **Remove and bring back the supplied system.** While another system exists and the build is not locked, Lolly Demo's card has **Remove**. Nothing is deleted: the system is hidden, stays the fallback, and returns with **Bring back** on its card or a `ds=shipped` link.
- **Editing Lolly Demo reads as editing in place.** Your first edit still makes a copy, but the copy now takes the supplied system's name and place in the list, and **Bring back the original Lolly Demo** is offered. On the blank starter a first edit still starts a design system of your own.
- **Reset to original.** A system's menu has **Reset to original** for an edited Lolly Demo (the copy is removed and the catalog's own system comes back) and for Lolly Starter (its original tokens and name come back). Systems made on this device, opened from a file, hosted or locked have no reset.
- **Managed means read-only.** In **Settings**, **Design systems**, a supplied system reads **Managed by this instance** only when it is locked, the one case where editing is not possible. Other supplied systems read **Came with this app**, or **From this instance** in an app connected to one.
- **The Design system tab reads like a magazine.** The tab opens on a cover: the logo, the name in the system's own display face, the author's description and a line of facts that doubles as contents. Then come Colour (ink and paper for both looks, every named colour with its hex and print ink, the spectrum, and the colour roles in a fold), Type (headings, text, emphasis and code, each in its own face), Logos (each file on the ground it was made for), Colourways, Tokens and Print & CMYK. The palette wheel, brand seal and type-in-motion illustrations are gone from this tab.
- **A strip of covers switches systems.** A row of covers at the top of the tab switches the active design system in place, with Undo. It works as one keyboard stop with the arrow keys, Home and End. The tab now refreshes when the design system changes anywhere else, where before it went stale. On a first visit the strip appears as soon as the second system arrives.
- **Compact Preferences rows.** **Preferences**, **Design systems** shows compact rows with small covers instead of large tiles. The colour count now matches everywhere: Lolly Demo shows 33 colours and Lolly Starter shows 9.
- **One name: Design System Setup.** The `#/start` surface was called five different things in the app. It is now **Design System Setup** in the profile menu, the welcome dialog, the Start page and the docs; "Make it yours" stays as its heading. The address does not change.
- **A Tokens room for first-timers.** The Tokens room opens with a plain introduction and a real example, section tiles with icons and counts, colour previews, a format menu, **Download**, **Copy CSS** and **Add from…**, and empty states that say what to do next.
- **Usage & rules checks text contrast.** A new **Text contrast** check feeds the existing **Needs changes** gate, using the WCAG large-text rule (3:1 at 24 px and up, or about 18.7 px bold, and 4.5:1 below that). The Title slide example now reads in light and dark, the chart example fills 16:9, and the mode menu names the mode the examples render.
- **Duplicate colours.** In Design System Setup, **Colours**, the selection bar gains **Duplicate**. Each copy is a colour of its own, named "<colour> copy", placed after its source, ready for you to move a shade, a tone or some chroma. One Undo takes back the whole batch.
- **Brand colours first in Assets.** **Assets**, **Swatches** always shows the brand's own colours. The role colours Lolly derives, and repeats of a colour already listed, sit behind a **Show colour roles** card at the foot of the section.

## House rules, creation feedback and brand skills

- **Brand skills.** A design system can carry brand skills: the brand team's own standards for one discipline, such as photography or motion, written as an Agent Skills `SKILL.md`. Lolly stores each with a permanent id, revision, checksum, licence and audience, serves it to agents on every surface and never rewrites the text. Design System Setup has a **Skills** room, Assets has a **Guidelines** group, and Share invitations include the skills that apply to the open tool. Internal skills never reach a static build, a hosted MCP server or a public brief.
- **"Guided by" in the export record.** When an agent was handed brand skills, the export records each skill's id, name, revision and checksum, and the export card shows **Guided by**, which you can untick. The wording is "guided by", never "complies with".
- **Creation feedback in Design.** Design's **Before you export** card now runs one report when the Export panel opens: the design system's house rules checked against the document, and a writing signal for the visible text with up to three flagged phrases and the layers they sit on. **Open the text in Verify** hands the text to Verify. The card informs and never blocks. Findings from a required rule are warnings and count toward "to fix"; findings from an advisory rule are notes. Hidden layers, and layers on hidden artboards or inside hidden groups, are left out and counted.
- **Lolly Demo has house rules.** On lolly.tools, Lolly Demo now states four approved house rules: headlines are never set in capitals (required), body text is left-aligned, primary marks go on light grounds and reverse marks on dark, and dashed lines mean a drop area (all three advisory). Design, `lolly check` and the Usage & rules room report real findings instead of saying the system states no rules.
- **Logo findings name the logo.** A logo finding now gives each logo's display name instead of its file id.

## Design

- **Large documents stay quick.** A Design canvas performance review led to a set of changes that do less work per frame and per edit on big documents. None of them changes an export:
  - the view and the address are written once a pan, pinch or zoom settles, not on every frame;
  - moving the view no longer rescans every card, and the compositor carries the motion;
  - at far zoom, artboards draw a lighter level of detail;
  - the inspector and navigator notice changes by identity instead of comparing whole documents;
  - sliders commit when you release them;
  - Undo keeps references instead of copies;
  - recovery drafts are bounded;
  - the history preview captures pictures at the size it draws them.
- **Large documents keep a pointer in the address.** When a document is too big for the address (over 256 KiB of readable content), the address keeps its place, view and saved slot instead of the whole document, and a reload restores the document from the saved copy. **Share** says plainly when a link cannot carry the document, points you at the `.lolly` file and offers a link that opens the saved copy on this device only.
- **Artboard titles when they help.** By default a title shows for selected artboards, the artboard that holds the selection, the one under the pointer and a title being renamed. The document menu has **Artboard titles: Always, Selected and hovered, Off**, beside **Rulers and guides**.
- **Step between artboards.** Page Down and Page Up frame the next and previous artboard, Home and End the first and last. Hold Z and drag to zoom to an area; Alt-click zooms out. Both are on the shortcut sheet.
- **Zoom keys.** Shift+0 zooms to 100% on every keyboard layout. `+` and `-` zoom about the pointer when it is over the canvas. The lowest zoom goes further out, and **Fit all** reads from the document.

## Write, and new files in Projects

- **Write.** A new utility for plain text and Markdown, with a file name, a format choice, word count, undo, find and replace, a Markdown preview, saving to Assets and downloads.
- **New file in Projects.** Projects gains **New file** in its toolbar, empty state, creation tile and background menu. Choose plain text or Markdown, name the file, and it opens in Write. The empty file is saved first, in the current folder. Write and Text both have **Save to Projects**, and their documents keep History like other project documents.
- **Safer Markdown preview.** The Markdown preview passes its HTML through DOMPurify, so a `javascript:` link is removed.

## URL Screenshot draws geometry

- **SVGs are drawings, not pictures.** URL Screenshot's SVG used to be a screenshot wrapped in an SVG on the web and in the terminal, and a print to PDF on desktop that turned filters, shadows, masks and emoji into bitmaps. The app's own SVG export walker now runs inside the captured page, and the result is checked against a screenshot taken at the same moment. Text is outlined from the fonts the page loaded. Only what has no vector form, such as canvas, video and embedded frames, becomes a picture of that region. A capture that differs from its screenshot too much falls back to the print on desktop and to the screenshot elsewhere.
- **Where the vector path runs.** The CLI and terminal app, the desktop app, and the web app with the Chrome extension 0.5.0 (after one approval). The hosted MCP service does not take this path.

## Collaboration

- **The reply is a link or a code.** When you join a private session, step 3 shows the reply link and the reply code side by side, each with its own copy button; send either back through any messaging app. The reply no longer shows a QR code, because the inviter's waiting screen has nothing that can scan one. The invite keeps its QR code, and nearby and rendezvous pairing are unchanged.
- **A big paste no longer makes you a viewer.** In a Work room, one insert into a long list rewrites every row's order. On documents of 500 or more rows that single gesture used to fill the outbox and turn its author into a viewer. The outbox now counts whole gestures, so insert, paste, duplicate and reordering work at any size, and a runaway is still stopped.

## The Mac app

- **A unified title bar.** On macOS the app's top row sits beside the window buttons. The app gains **File**, **Open…** and **Open Recent**, and a Dock menu.

## Look and feel

- **One collapsible card.** Tool sidebar sections, the Assets inspector and Settings cards are now one component with one opening motion, so Settings cards no longer jump or shake as they open.
- **Links and checkboxes in the house style.** App links that no style claimed were drawn blue and purple by the browser, and many checkboxes were grey native ticks. They now use the app's own link colour and the shared tick. Tool canvases and exports are unchanged.
- **Polish.** Inset inputs, upload areas, stage controls, gallery surfaces and the template chooser cards are refined. The History panel loses its extra padding, and a panel docked to the window edge has square corners.

## Also in this release

- **Isolated tools work in Safari.** In production builds, Safari started a second copy of the hook worker's message listener, so every tool that runs its logic in a worker (such as Snippet) drew an empty card. Each worker now binds one listener.
- **The WebGPU check finishes on slow machines.** On a slow machine with software graphics, the gallery's looping animations kept the WebGPU startup check from finishing within its 30 s, and the app showed its unsupported card on a machine that does support WebGPU. The gallery now holds its motion until the check settles.
- **The export check states the size it writes.** The export card's **Size** fact now states the size the export writes, where it used to state the tool's default canvas when the size fields were filled in by the tool.
- **3-D previews stop when they leave the page.** 3D Studio 1.3.1, Booth Studio 0.3.2, Chart 1.27.1, Flythrough 0.2.4 and Timezone 1.1.1 stop their preview loops once their canvas is removed.

## Documentation

- **Rewritten for readers.** Every English guide was reviewed for accuracy and rewritten for the people who read it: 553 confirmed errors were corrected, links to `.md` files fixed, private material removed and the internal runbooks moved to the repositories. The translated sites carry the new pages in all 26 languages.
- **Screenshots in Lolly Demo.** All docs screenshots are now captured under Lolly Demo, the design system lolly.tools runs, and the recapture found and fixed several app bugs.
- **New pages.** Brand skills, a creators page for colourways, a visual Lolly Demo section in Start, and Work guides for running on AWS or MulgaDC Spinifex (a review draft), OpenTelemetry export, per-group feature flags, metrics and alerts, admission policy with Kubewarden, and K3s on arm64 and transactional hosts.

## For operators

- **Lolly Work 1.1.3.** Pairs with this release. Read the Work release notes for its own changes, among them the appliance image, the podman profile, `lolly-update`, OpenTelemetry export, per-group feature flags and an opt-in pull dispatch for render workers.
- **The web app trusts the Work instance that serves it.** A release web app pins the publisher's catalog key, while Lolly Work signs each person's catalog with the installation's own key, so a published web app served by Work showed no tools. The web app now also accepts the key the serving Work instance publishes at `/api/v1/catalog/public-key`, on the same origin only, and only for a catalog envelope signed with that key. The desktop and mobile apps never use an instance key, and a static host never sends the request. This needs Work 1.1.3; the interim patch in the ready-made package comes out once both are deployed.
- **MCP limits are settings.** The MCP server's size limits and one-request rules used to depend on the `VERCEL` variable, so they did not apply on the K3s host lolly.tools has used since 7 October. They now depend on whether the server is hosted, with three settings: `LOLLY_MCP_REQUEST_MAX_BYTES` (default 4,500,000), `LOLLY_MCP_RESPONSE_MAX_BYTES` (default 4,400,000) and `LOLLY_MCP_MAX_IMAGE_BYTES` (default 4 MiB). The sovereign chart sets them explicitly.
- **One version.** `lolly --version`, the CLI's render envelope and the npm packages now carry the release number, 1.1.3. Both Helm charts in this repository move to chart version 1.1.3 with `appVersion` 1.1.3.
- **Catalog signing.** `scripts/sign-catalog.ts --min-seq <n>` (or `LOLLY_CATALOG_MIN_SEQ`) raises the signed sequence, so a catalog signed with a new key starts above the sequence browsers already hold.
- **Routes and packages.** The installation routes table marks the Evroc module, the Dokploy blueprint and the UpCloud infrastructure module as not ready until each has an acceptance. The `lolly-web` RPM is marked not maintained until OBS builds return. Preview images are now signed too.
- **Companion design systems.** `validate:catalog` checks a catalog's `companionDesignSystems` list: each entry must name a known tokens asset or the bundled starter, never the `brandTokens` asset, with no duplicates.

## For tool authors and builders

- **Brand skills.** A new asset type, `skill` (id `<ns>/skill/<slug>`, files at `<ns>/skills/<slug>/SKILL.md`). The engine admits a skill only without scripts, executables, path traversal or invalid UTF-8, within its size limits and with at most 16 reference files. It compares a skill with the design system's tokens deterministically, with no model involved. The CLI adds `lolly system skills`, `lolly system skill <name> [--into] [--mode=linked|bundled]`, `skill coverage` and `skill check <folder>`. MCP serves `lolly://design-system/skills` and each skill as resources, and the design brief gains a `skills` section.
- **Creation feedback.** `creationFeedback(boxes, rules, doc, opts)` returns house-rule findings, the writing score and the hidden layers left out, with a plain summary. `designVisibleText` reads the drawn words in reading order, and `creationFeedbackDelta` gives the compact answer after one edit. In the app's agent tools, `lolly_apply_document_changes` reports feedback after each change and the read-only `lolly_check_document` returns the full report. `lolly check` and MCP `lolly_check` include the writing score, as information only.
- **House rules and hidden layers.** `checkDesignHouseRules` now leaves out a layer whose artboard, group or layout container is hidden, at any depth, where it used to check only the layer's own flag.

## Dependencies and security

- **Certificate checks.** `rustls-webpki` 0.103.16 and the webpki root crates 1.0.10 fix RUSTSEC-2026-0381, RUSTSEC-2026-0382 and RUSTSEC-2026-0383 in both apps.
- **Pinned past advisories.** `zod` 4.6.7 and `undici` 8.12.3 are pinned past GHSA-mrhx-c7xc-m3w5 and GHSA-xjj6-f33q-929c.
- **Zip advisories still held for Windows builds.** RUSTSEC-2026-0367, RUSTSEC-2026-0368 and RUSTSEC-2026-0369 still affect `zip` 4.6.1 through `tauri-plugin-updater` 2.14.0, compiled for Windows only. They stay recorded exceptions until the updater plugin ships its fix. Self-update is off.
- **Library updates.** The font subset adapter moves to skera 0.9.0 with write-fonts 0.55.0 (its worker asset grows from 671 KB to 890 KB). The desktop app's D-Bus services move to zbus 6, and ONNX Runtime bindings to `ort` 2.0.0-rc.15. Vite 8.3.5 and the Tauri CLI 2.12.4 reach both apps.

## Known limits

- **Pairing without a channel.** Two devices with no messaging app, nearby or rendezvous between them can no longer finish private pairing on the web: the reply code is about 100 to 125 characters, too long to type.
- **URL Screenshot through MCP.** The hosted MCP service does not draw URL Screenshot's vector SVG.
- **Brand skills are read, not written.** Lolly stores and serves brand skills; writing or editing one happens outside Lolly.

**Why four platforms stay held.** Lolly needs WebGPU, and the 29 October qualification (lolly #588) found no platform ready:

- **Linux:** WebKitGTK 2.52.6 has no WebGPU at all. A hands-on check needs a WebKitGTK built with WebGPU.
- **Windows:** WebView2 155 offers the WebGPU API, but found no adapter on a CI machine without a GPU.
- **Android:** the Android 16 emulator offers the API, but found no adapter on a CI machine without a GPU.
- **macOS Intel:** the Intel Mac CI machine runs macOS 15.7, whose web view has no WebGPU.

Each needs a hands-on check on real hardware before its row can change.
