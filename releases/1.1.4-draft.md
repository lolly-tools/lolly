# Lolly 1.1.4: the springy one

These notes cover the work merged to `main` after [v1.1.3](https://github.com/lolly-tools/lolly/releases/tag/v1.1.3) (`e3a35cc88`, 29 October 2026) up to 3 November 2026: 121 changes on the main line (356 commits), including the four Storytold adoption batches, much of it merged straight to `main` while hosted CI was paused. The engine moves from 1.285.0 to 1.301.0. The tool-author core stays at 1.5.0. Items marked **In review** are on open pull requests and ship only if they are merged before the release is cut.

Everything you install carries one number, as it has since 1.1.3: the apps, the web image and archive, the MCP and CA services, the CLI and terminal app, the Penpot plugins, Lolly Work with its render worker and Helm chart, and the YunoHost packages. The SDK (`@lolly-tools/core`) and the engine keep their own version numbers, because they describe the tool-author interface rather than something people install.

## Open files from other apps

- **Native InDesign files (preview).** Design and the CLI open `.indd` files directly (`lolly adobe indd`), converting them on your device to IDML and then using the IDML import. The import report labels the result a preview. In the web app the conversion runs in the background, can be cancelled, and refuses files that are not InDesign. 154 of 154 open-licence test files convert.
- **A fuller IDML reader.** Styles, text, master pages, tables and more kinds of object carry over from IDML, and what Lolly approximates is listed in the import report.
- **Affinity files.** Design, Unpack and the CLI open `.af`, `.afdesign`, `.afphoto`, `.afpub` and `.aftemplate` (container versions 8 to 12). Artboards become frames, and groups, rectangles, paths, fills, strokes, text, tables and pictures become editable layers. Effects, adjustments, masks and master pages are listed in the import report, not applied. When the layers cannot be mapped, the file's own preview opens as one picture, with a note saying so. `lolly affinity` prints a file's facts, or writes its preview or its Design layers. All 51 public test files open and map.
- **DXF drawings.** Design and `lolly dxf` open ASCII and binary DXF, R12 to 2018, with one group per layer and units taken from the drawing. Hatches, external references, paper space and SHX fonts are reported, never guessed.
- **Editing timelines.** Design import and the CLI read CMX 3600 EDL, OpenTimelineIO and FCPXML 1.8 to 1.11 into the same sequence as Premiere XML, including drop-frame 29.97 and 59.94 timecode. Transitions, speed changes, effects, markers and nested clips are reported.
- **Spreadsheets read as Excel shows them.** Dates, currency, percentages and fractions in an imported spreadsheet show as Excel formats them, in the Data view, in data inputs and in the CLI. The format engine runs on your device as a small WebAssembly module.
- **Newer PSD parser.** The PSD parser moves from 0.2.0 to 0.18.0, with memory bounds, allocation caps and overflow-safe fixes. The corpus run still admits and preserves all 258 files.
- Lolly now opens 61 formats.

## Print and PDF

- **CMYK through a press profile.** Print PDF, Print TIFF and EPS (CMYK) separate through the bundled Coated FOGRA39 profile, or through your own embedded profile, instead of a formula. Greys stay on the black plate and black still overprints. FOGRA51, SWOP and GRACoL keep the formula.
- **A PDF accessibility report.** For a PDF, the CLI's `--export-report` lists 32 rule results: passed, failed, not applicable, or needs a person. The wording never claims that a PDF is accessible or compliant.

## Audio, captions and pictures

- **Repair and measure audio.** Clean 1.1.0 reports the full EBU R128 loudness numbers before and after: momentary, short-term, integrated, loudness range, sample peak and true peak. (The old "True peak" figure was the sample peak.) "Repair without a model" handles background noise, mains hum, sibilance, clicks and reverb, and the CLI repairs WAV files the same way. Long clips run in the background.
- **Broadcast captions.** Captions 1.1.0 exports `.ttml` (IMSC 1.1 Text) and `.scc` (CEA-608 pop-on at 29.97 drop-frame) beside SRT and VTT, and reads both back. On the CLI they are `--export=ttml` and `--export=scc`. Characters the 608 set cannot carry are refused, never dropped.
- **Trace an image in Design.** Right-click an image and choose **Trace image…**, with Logo, Illustration, Line art and Centreline presets. The trace runs as a job you can cancel, follows the box's fit so a cropped image traces only what shows, and puts the shapes in a group above the image.
- **Clean edge colour for mattes.** A new switch in the matte dialog (and `lolly matte --clean-edge-colour`) replaces the old background's colour in soft edges with the subject's own. It is off by default, and with it off the output is unchanged.
- **Darkroom reads more of a Camera Raw preset:** the colour mixer, relative temperature and tint, grayscale, grain, sharpening and darkening vignettes.

## Motion and animation

- **Springs for keyframes.** An ease can now be a spring, with presets, and the Keyframes popup lets you tune stiffness, damping and mass. A spring ends exactly on the next keyframe, and exports to dotLottie are baked into plain keys, so any player shows the same motion.
- **Eases that follow the design system.** A keyframe can bind its ease to a motion token by name, so changing the token changes every animation bound to that token. Lolly Demo and Lolly Starter come with a small motion scale to start from.
- **Edit the motion path on the canvas.** Drag handles on a position keyframe to curve the travel between two points, or edit the same handles from the Keyframes popup. Right-click a point on the path for the box's menu. dotLottie files carry the curve as native tangents.
- **Key one property at a time.** Fields show a diamond, the popup can filter to one property, and time can be shown in the units you prefer.
- **Animate how a shape looks.** Fill and stroke colour, stroke width, corner radius and trim can all be keyframed. They show in the preview, in video and still exports, and as native animated properties in dotLottie.
- **Steadier editing.** Several quick animation edits are all kept, and a failed edit no longer leaves later edits waiting.

## Design

- **Stroke position and per-side widths.** A stroke can sit inside, centred or outside a shape, and a rectangle can have a different width on each side. SVG, PDF and the Penpot round trip keep them; dotLottie refuses what Lottie cannot draw and says so.
- **A better pen.** Shift steps the angle, nodes snap, open paths carry on, handles retract, and a Pencil tool shares the Pen's place in the rail.
- **Text keeps its kerning.** A list marker and its text, or a coloured span beside plain text, are shaped together so the spacing matches the browser. A stretch in one face with a ligature across a span stays in its slot.
- **Shortcuts and tokens.** Shift+1, Shift+2 and Shift+0 work while an artboard title has focus. A picked swatch changes the selected gradient stop straight away, and an open token list stays open through Link token.
- **Quicker.** Changing the chrome no longer restyles the whole page.

## Your colours

- **Choose your colourway in Settings.** **Settings**, **Appearance** lets you pick a colourway. The app's chrome follows it, and new work starts in that colourway.
- **Make a colourway in the Colours room.** Create and edit colourways in Brand Studio, and the Type room shows the design system's own faces. A design system that chose its own faces owns all of them.
- **Logos say where they belong.** Logo and icon grounds follow the colour scheme, including the Brand theme, and tiles show each logo on the surface it was made for. Design System Setup shows the supplied system's logos instead of "Not set".
- **An empty colour means automatic.** An unset colour field reads as automatic and never as black, and Certificate, Timezone and Chart say what an empty colour draws.

## Brand Studio

- **One compact bar.** The bar and room strip take no more height than the old header on a desk, and on a phone the rooms sit in a bottom bar. Export and **Tokens (.json)** moved into the bar and the More menu.
- **A Board that shows what you have.** The Board shows a supplied system, Tokens tiles count what is really there, pale swatches keep an edge, and Type cards wrap and say their role.
- **Rules is called Rules.** The room was Usage & rules, and the skills check now points to the right room.
- **Phone layout.** Four colours a row, logo slots two to a row, and Tokens tiles that wrap.

## Tools

- **Quote (new).** A sales quote from a table of line items, with each line's total, a subtotal, a discount, named tax and the amount due in the chosen currency. Pasted tables are read by their headings and amounts are read the way spreadsheets write them. An empty price is "Priced later" and stays out of the totals, and a quantity of `?` holds the totals until it is set. One page lays itself out as a landscape page, a portrait sheet or a 3:1 screen with the totals beside the lines.
- **Snippet 1.7.** Colourways and Mode now change the code window, and the style controls sit in one Style section: Theme, Colourway and Mode, then Frame style, Window chrome and Drop shadow.
- **Certificate 1.2.1.** Colourway styles, every size, motion, and a print language that sets the direction. A typed size sizes the name for the sheet it makes, and Accent help lists what it paints in each style.
- **Logo Wall 1.8.0.** Draws as one SVG with sponsors grouped into tiers, keeps tier headings in proportion, draws brick rows at one logo size, offers an example look with placeholder wordmarks, lays itself out again for an export at another shape, and sets tier headings in the design system's heading face.
- **Agenda.** Colourways recolour the programme, defaults follow the brand, styles are more distinct, and motion is staged.
- **3D Studio.** Distinct marks for Tilt, Turn and Lean, and two-tone icons as a colour pair.
- **Music player, Stationery and Battlecards.** The brand mark is the design system's mono-reverse logo, Stationery's letterhead prints its logo, and Battlecards sizes are pixels.
- **Tools know the canvas size.** Every hook is told the size the tool is being made at. Logo Wall and others use the size in the web app, the CLI, the TUI and MCP, and an MCP render follows the requested height.
- **A tool can put its design-system choices in its own section.** A new manifest field lets a tool show the colourway and mode controls inside one of its sections, as Snippet does. Tools that do not use the field look the same as before.
- **Pictures a tool ships.** Asset inputs can read a picture that comes with a tool.

## Sharing

- **Rendered links whose recipients can change the inputs.** On a Lolly Work instance, the Share dialog's **Rendered link** row has an opt-in **Recipients can change the inputs** box. Anyone with that link can put new inputs in its address and get a newly rendered image; locked inputs stay as set. The box appears only for people who may also render on the server, and is off by default.
- **In review (#624): share links after Send.** After sending a file to Google Drive, Nextcloud, an S3 bucket, Dropbox or OneDrive, the Share dialog can make a link others can open, through the provider itself. It says who can open the link, where the file lives and when the link expires, and a "Links you've shared" list can stop sharing. Drive needs no new permission. Nextcloud links can carry a password and an expiry. S3 links last 1 to 7 days and cannot be stopped early. Dropbox asks for its sharing permission only the first time you make a link. OneDrive stays on its narrow app-folder permission and hands you to OneDrive's own Share page if Microsoft refuses. Lolly keeps no copy and no token on a server.

## Desktop, AI and self-hosting

- **Crash-safe saves in the desktop and mobile apps.** Documents, slots and migrations are written to a temporary file, synced and renamed, so a crash or a full disk leaves the old document intact. Stale temporary files are swept at startup.
- **A dependency advisory fixed.** The desktop updater moves to tauri-plugin-updater 2.14.1, which resolves a high-severity `zip` advisory (RUSTSEC-2026-0429) and the three held since 17 October. No audit exception is held.
- **Text tools answer quickly in the CLI, TUI and MCP.** A text tool's worker now loads only the text module instead of the whole engine, so it starts in a few hundred milliseconds rather than several seconds. Text Helper no longer times out on a theme switch.
- **In review: agents on this computer.** The desktop app can find the Claude Code and Codex command-line tools installed on the same machine and use them, with your own sign-in and subscription, to edit the open Design document. Each edit is one undo step and the agent shows in People. Nothing is detected until you open the panel, the app says where your document goes before first use, and a managed install can switch the feature off. No MCP server is needed on your computer.
- **In review (#625): keep the on-device models on your own server.** A YunoHost install can fetch the on-device model files once, check each one's size and checksum, and serve them itself, so the app stops reaching lolli.li. It needs a release built with this option.

## Everyday use

- **Performance UI switches itself on.** On a device that struggles, Performance UI turns on for good on a weak one and for a while on a constrained one. Settings says when and why, and an Undo offers **Allow again**. A switch in one tab never overwrites a choice made in another.
- **A tidier asset inspector.** Technical details merge into one Details section with labelled glyphs, dates share one style for your locale, a catalog PDF's or font's facts show straight away, and on a phone you open them from the dialog header. Uploaded images file under Photos and emoji sets under Emoji sets.
- **Verify reads better.** The Verify panel leads with "You made this here", and "Made from" choices are readable. Text you typed stays a fingerprint, with a line saying why.
- **Link exports follow the export panel's size.** A link export writes the size the panel shows, after the tool settles.
- **Faster first run.** Direct tool links open faster, repeated copy is gone, and the footer's Open button works from the moment it shows.
- **Undo toast with a second button.** A notice can wrap and carry a second button.
- **Cascadia fonts.** Cascadia Code and Cascadia Mono join the catalogue list. Aptos has no open twin, so it gets a plain "not available" line.
- **Model licences.** Each model family's stated licence is kept in a hand-written record that the offer sheet and Profile read.

## Command line, terminal and MCP

- **New commands and formats.** `lolly adobe indd`, `lolly affinity` and `lolly dxf` open those files; `--export-report` reports on a PDF's accessibility; `--export=ttml` and `--export=scc` write broadcast captions; and `lolly matte --clean-edge-colour` cleans soft edges.
- **Pipe pictures into lists.** The CLI fills a list that takes dropped files from piped pictures, which is how a sponsor wall gets its logos. `describe` shows the picture flags, and the TUI resolves picture paths from a CSV.
- **Safer picture handling.** A picture must be a whole signature, so text that only starts like one is refused. A refused picture's URL is shown without its query or user name, the TUI refuses a pasted link that points at local files, and hosted decode limits are in force.
- **Still exports hold the right frame.** A still export leaves a tool's own export preparation alone and poses only a frame clock that declares a still pose. PNG sizes its SVG from the drawing's root `<svg>`.
- **Design pages in the terminal.** The TUI and MCP record Design pages drawn from operations.
- **A check on MCP labels.** A test fails if a tool that writes is advertised as read-only. No tool was mislabelled.
- **`ci:local` for contributors.** `pnpm run ci:local` runs the gates that CI runs for a pull request, in order, with path filters. Lanes that only run on the hosted service are reported as skipped.

## Documents and languages

- **New guides.** Working with partners (with five vector shots), the Brand Studio's bar, Board and Rules, springs and motion paths, per-property keyframing, the automatic Performance UI switch, making a colourway your own, brand architectures with a chooser and "What to do today" recipes, content sovereignty with connected DAM workflows, tracing an image, and broadcast captions.
- **Coolify for Lolly Work.** The install guides cover Coolify beside a VM, UpCloud and Dokploy.
- **26 languages.** The new and changed strings and docs are carried into all 26 languages, including the landing page.
- **A new landing page.** People-door cards that start from who you are, a truer hero, a terminal row, Certificate and Logo Wall as cover examples, a Verify badge that says what it proves, and no Intel Mac tile on the download rail.

## Lolly Work

Work 1.1.4 pairs the Work server and render worker with this Lolly release. Since 1.1.3, many Work pull requests are re-pins to newer Lolly commits and documentation syncs. What you may notice:

- **HTML-layout tools render in the Chromium worker.** Tools that lay out HTML render on the server through the render worker, and an instance without a worker says so.
- **Open links.** A share, embed or download link to a tool can be minted as open, so its recipients can change the tool's inputs and get a newly rendered asset within the tool's policy.
- **Worker renders keep design token colours.**
- **Coolify.** A project-owned Compose recipe for Coolify, with Coolify's proxy, generated secrets and domain variable wired. It has not yet been imported into a running Coolify, and it is not in Coolify's one-click catalogue.
- **YunoHost lists tools.** The YunoHost package signs each installation's catalogue, which makes the tools appear in the app (1.1.3~ynh2), and the `full_domain` key that YunoHost 13 refuses is gone.
- **Appliance and ready-made package.** The 1.1.2 to 1.1.3 update was accepted against the published chart, and the x86_64 appliance was accepted on Spinifex. The ready-made package pins Work and Lolly 1.1.3.
- **In review (lolly-work #272): Nextcloud on the same YunoHost, and a Garage store.** The YunoHost package finds a Nextcloud on the same server and prefills a connection in its settings, which becomes a brand-file source and a delivery folder once an admin pastes an app password. Compose gains an optional Garage service as the file store.
- **In review: Bunny and Cloudflare edge adapters.** An instance can name Bunny or Cloudflare in front of its public files, with purge by tag and checks that the provider's image and page rewriting is off. Under the default EU-only rule, Cloudflare may front only a hostname that serves public files. No CDN is switched on for lolly.tools or lolly.ing.

Work's version and chart numbers move with Lolly's at release.

## Known limits

- **Native InDesign import is a preview.** The clean-room caveats are recorded in the module's `UPSTREAM.md`.
- **Affinity effects are reported, not applied.** Effects, adjustments, masks and master pages appear in the import report only.
- **Lolly's own PDFs fail the tagging checks.** The accessibility report is honest about this: Lolly's exporters write no PDF tags yet.
- **FOGRA39 is ICC 4.3.** A Print PDF states the print condition without claiming PDF/X-4.
- **Translations of batch 1.** 26 translations of the edited `docs/exporting.md`, `design-import.md` and `formats` paragraphs from the first Storytold batch need the usual regeneration pass. Batches 2 to 4 carried their own translations.
- **Platforms stay held.** Intel Macs are deprecated, and Windows, Linux and Android wait for a hands-on WebGPU check on real hardware, as in 1.1.3.
- **Hosted CI was paused** from 30 October and the Actions quota ran out, so qualification for this release must be rerun before the release is cut.
