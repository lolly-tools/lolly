The mark

Three rings, three parties

A candy no one could pull by hand: three nested rings, where most lollies have one rope. You sit at the centre, Lolly holds the middle and the outer ring is Lolly at massive scale.

  1. Inner ring · YouThe person at the centre, with choice, power and creative freedom.
  2. Middle ring · LollyThe engine and the studio around you: the same inputs always make the same file.
  3. Outer ring · At ScaleLolly at massive scale: every person, team and file at once.
  • Bunyapine is the mark's green
  • Spearmint carries the reverse and mono marks
  • The wordmark is Liquorice on light, Daylight reversed and Green Apple on brand
  • Lolly's tone takes the guest's hue
  • Never a stick, a dish or a tilt under the mark
  • The stick appears only in small line icons and when Fauna holds the lolly

Crossland · type

Crossland: three faces, one voice

Crossland is a tribute to Dave Crossland, who has spent years making the case for free fonts (see Shoulders of Giants), and to collaboration and cooperation across the countries we each call home. Outfit gives Lolly a headline voice of its own. SUSE carries the words and the italics, and SUSE Mono reads the code: enough of a nod to where Lolly comes from.

  • SUSE for reading, and for every italic

  • No faux italics: Outfit has none

Etiquette · icons

Etiquette: good manners in a 24 pixel square

Etiquette is Lolly's icon set, drawn in the open line style of Lucide, at the same weight as the words beside each icon.

The lolly glyph · the one icon with a stick
  • 24 px grid, 2 px stroke, round caps and joins
  • One line weight, drawn open, in the colour of the words beside each icon
  • The lolly glyph leans candy top-right, stick bottom-left, at 45°
  • No other icon carries a stick

Motion and Calm

Still, until something happens

Lolly moves when you ask for something, and stands still when you ask for calm. Calm and Reduce motion show the still of every moving piece.

Try Calm: this page's colourful pictures quieten to half saturation and half contrast, and the swirl and the kangaroo hold still.

  • At most one thing moving
  • Only while something is happening
  • Calm and Reduce motion show stills
  • Jelly is opt-in
