# Free background music generator

Need background music for a video, a podcast, a stream, a game or a presentation? Lolly's [music generator](/#/music) makes a new track in any of more than 50 genres, from lofi and jazz to synthwave and drum and bass, right on your device. Music you make is free for any use, including commercial use, as long as you credit Lolly with the line **Music made with Lolly**.

## Free with credit

Music you make in the music generator is free for any use, including commercial use, provided you credit Lolly. Put this line wherever your credits go:

> **Music made with Lolly**

When there is room for a link, add the address: **Music made with Lolly (https://lolly.tools)**. Put the credit in a video's description or end credits, a podcast's show notes, a stream's description, a game's credits screen, or a presentation's last slide.

Looking for copyright-free background music? Music made here is not copyright-free, because the credit is a condition of using the music. The music is free to use, even commercially, and royalty-free as long as you include the credit.

## Made on your device, with no AI

- **No samples, no loops, no recordings.** Lolly's composer writes every note, and instruments modelled in code play them. Even the drum breaks, the birds and the waves are synthesised.
- **No generative AI.** The composer is a set of rules for each genre, not a trained model, and it makes the same tune from the same genre and seed on every device.
- **Content Credentials in every file.** Each M4A or WAV you download carries a signed record that says Lolly's music generator made the music, procedurally and with no AI. The record's digital source type is algorithmic media.
- **Nothing to sign up for.** The music generator runs in your browser. Your tracks stay on your device until you share them.

## Questions

### Is the music free?

Yes. The music generator costs nothing to use, and music you make in the generator is free for any use, as long as you credit Lolly.

### Can I use the music commercially?

Yes. You can use music you make in a paid product, an advert, a monetised video or a client project, as long as you credit Lolly.

### Do I need to credit Lolly?

Yes. The credit is the one condition. Use the line **Music made with Lolly**, and add **https://lolly.tools** when there is room for a link.

### Is the music copyright-free?

No, and the difference matters. Because the credit is a condition, the music is not copyright-free. The music is free to use, even commercially, and royalty-free as long as you include the credit.

### Is the music made with AI?

No. Lolly's composer follows rules written for each genre, and it plays the notes on instruments modelled in code. No trained model writes or plays anything. Every downloaded file records that in its Content Credentials as algorithmic media, so a checker such as [Lolly Verify](/#/verify) does not flag the music as AI.

### Does the music use samples or loops?

No. Every sound is synthesised by Lolly's own code as the track plays: the drums, the voices, the birds and the waves included. No recording of anyone's music is in a track.

### What file types can I download?

M4A (AAC), a small file that plays almost anywhere, and WAV, an uncompressed file for editing and mastering. Each file carries Content Credentials. To remove them, run the file through the [Lolly Strip Hidden Data](/t/strip-data) utility.

### Can I make a longer track or a loop?

Yes. Tune sets the length of the track in bars, and a track ends the way a song does, with its own outro. Save the track to your assets and pick the tune as a video's music bed in Lolly, and the tune loops to fill the video.

### Which browsers does the music generator work in?

Current Chrome and Edge, Safari 26 or later, and Firefox with WebGPU turned on. [Supported environments](/info/supported-environments.html) has the details. The samples on these pages play in any browser.
