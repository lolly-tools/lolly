# Lolly for legal teams

A file about to leave the building is often a PDF full of names, a screenshot with a face in it, or a document whose hidden metadata says more than its pages do. Lolly cleans it on the device it is already on, with no upload, no account and no server in the middle.

## Nothing leaves to be cleaned

- **Black out text permanently.** [Redact](/#/tool/redact) rebuilds the file around the boxes you draw, so the words under them are gone rather than covered, and it verifies the output before it downloads. PDFs and images alike.
- **Anonymise the text.** The de-identify mode in [Text](/#/tool/text-helper) swaps the personal identifiers in a block of text for placeholders, so an interview, a log or an export can be shared without identifying the people named in the text.
- **Strip what the pages do not show.** [Strip Hidden Data](/#/tool/strip-data) reveals and removes the metadata riding inside images and PDFs: locations, devices, authors, edit history.
- **Shrink without sending.** [Compress](/#/tool/compress-pdf) recompresses a PDF's images on the device. A "free online compressor" would mean uploading the file to someone else's server.
- **Check what you were sent.** [Verify](/#/verify) reads a file's Content Credential where you are: who made it, with what, and whether AI was involved. The walk-through is [Verify it yourself](/info/verify-yourself.html).

Every one of these is a utility in the app, listed together under [Utilities](/#/utilities), and every one of them works with the network cable pulled.

## The positions, stated plainly

- **Licence.** MPL-2.0, with no contributor agreement. What that means for an organisation is in [Adoption & Governance](/info/adoption-governance.html).
- **AI marking.** What Lolly declares about AI-assisted files, how, and what it does not claim, in [AI marking and the EU AI Act](/info/eu-ai-act.html).
- **Data.** Ordinary editing, rendering and downloads stay on your device. Sharing, Sync, hosted rendering and lolly.work send what they need to the server you or your organisation use. The details are in [Privacy](/info/privacy.html) and [Trust](/info/trust.html).
