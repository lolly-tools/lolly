# Lolly for legal teams

A file about to be shared is often a PDF full of names, a screenshot with a face in it or a document whose hidden metadata says more than its pages do. Lolly cleans the file on the device it is already on, with no upload, no account and no server.

## Clean a file without sending it anywhere

- **Black out text permanently.** [Lolly Redact](/t/redact) rebuilds the file around the boxes you draw. The words under the boxes are gone, not covered. Redact verifies the output before the download. It works on PDFs and images.
- **Anonymise the text.** The de-identify mode in [Lolly Text](/t/text-helper) replaces the personal identifiers in a block of text with placeholders. An interview, a log or an export can then be shared without identifying the people it names.
- **Strip what the pages do not show.** [Lolly Strip Hidden Data](/t/strip-data) reveals and removes the metadata inside images, audio files and PDFs: locations, devices, authors, edit history, tags and Content Credentials.
- **Shrink without sending.** [Lolly Pages](/t/pages?operation=compress) recompresses a PDF's images on the device. A free online compressor would upload the file to someone else's server.
- **Check what you were sent.** [Lolly Verify](/#/verify) reads a file's Content Credentials on your device: who made it, with what and whether AI was involved. [Verify it yourself](/info/verify-yourself.html) walks through the steps.

Each one is a utility in the app, listed under [Utilities](/#/utilities), and each one works offline.

## The positions, stated plainly

- **Licence.** MPL-2.0, with no contributor agreement. [Adoption & Governance](/info/adoption-governance.html) explains what that means for an organisation.
- **Creative rights.** Lolly records the licence of each source a file uses and prepares the credits it owes. A `.lolly` file holds back catalog material whose recorded licence is not yet interpreted, unless you choose to include that material. Material with no licence recorded travels with a warning, and the file's `CREDITS.txt` lists what travelled and asks the recipient to check the terms of anything without a recorded licence. Lolly does not clear rights. See [Creative rights and credits](/info/creative-rights.html).
- **AI marking.** [AI marking and the EU AI Act](/info/eu-ai-act.html) sets out what Lolly declares about AI-assisted files, how Lolly declares it and what Lolly does not claim.
- **Data.** Ordinary editing, rendering and downloads stay on your device. Sharing, Sync, hosted rendering and Lolly Work send what they need to the server you or your organisation use. [Privacy](/info/privacy.html) and [Trust](/info/trust.html) have the details.
