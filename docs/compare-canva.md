# Lolly and Canva

_Last checked: 28 September 2026._

If your team makes social posts, slides and flyers in Canva, this page says where Lolly does the same job and where Canva is the better fit.

<!--i:eti-award-->
## What Canva does better

Canva combines a large template and stock library, apps and hosted team workflows in one managed service. The ready-made content helps when you need a starting point today. See [Canva's templates](https://www.canva.com/templates/) and [apps marketplace](https://www.canva.com/apps/). Canva's live editing has a long operating history at a scale Lolly's work collabs have not reached.

<!--i:eti-blend-->
## Where they overlap

Both let a non-designer start from a template and produce a finished graphic. Both support team work and brand controls. [Canva packages these in its business plans](https://www.canva.com/canva-business/). Lolly keeps the creative app and the organisation server apart.

<!--i:eti-lolly-->
## How Lolly does the overlapping job

A Lolly tool sets the changes people may make. The same tool runs in the browser, the desktop app or the CLI. A layout made on the Lolly Design canvas can become a [tool with rules](/info/create-a-tool.html). One tool can make many files from a table of values.

![Lolly Design's free canvas, where the colours, faces and assets on offer are the design system's own](/t/url-shot?url=%2F%23%2Ftool%2Fdesign%3Ftemplate%3D__blank__&width=1440&height=900&dpi=192&waitMs=2400&walker=1&format=svg&dark=1&filename=aud-open-canvas)

Standalone Lolly makes and exports on the device, with no account. It works offline once its assets are saved. Two people can edit live in a [private collab](/info/collaborate.html), device to device. Content Credentials are on by default for supported exports, and you can turn them off.

## With an organisation

Lolly Work, run on your organisation's own server, adds sign-in, shared projects, live work collabs, approved assets and policies. [What runs where](/info/compare.html#what-runs-where) sets the two parts side by side, with the limits every comparison shares.
