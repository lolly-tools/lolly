# Meet Lolly Demo

Lolly Demo is Lolly's own brand and the design system lolly.tools opens with. Your brand is the show. Lolly is the Studio.

::: figure lolly-demo-mark
The Lolly mark and wordmark. Three rings: you at the centre, Lolly in the middle, Lolly at scale on the outside.
:::

::: figure lolly-demo-parts
Five parts, each with a name. Choose one to open its standards.
:::

## Opal and its colourways

::: figure lolly-demo-opal
Every Opal colour has a name you can remember instead of a number. Green leads everywhere Lolly owns, and six colourways bring other moods.
:::

## Crossland and Etiquette

::: figure lolly-demo-type
Crossland sets headings in Outfit, text and italics in SUSE, and code in SUSE Mono. Etiquette draws one 2 px line on a 24 px grid, and only the lolly glyph has a stick.
:::
