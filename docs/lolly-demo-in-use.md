## Colourways on real work

Pick a colourway for each piece in the tool's **Design system** section. The export records your choice.

![A QR code in Lolly Demo: the colourway dots in the tool's Design system section, and the export panel recording Lolly Demo and the Spearmint colourway](/info/shots/export-panel.svg)

::: figure lolly-demo-in-use-colourways
Choose a card to try that colourway in a link card, with Lolly Demo active.
:::
