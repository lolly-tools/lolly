# Lolly Demo in use

Where each part of Lolly Demo appears while you work, and what travels inside the files you export.

::: figure lolly-demo-in-use-map
The app wears Lolly. Your file wears the active design system and its colourway, with a record and Content Credentials inside. Lolly shows itself at the register: the render, the export, the send.
:::

## Colourways on real work

Pick a colourway for each piece in the tool's **Design system** section. The export records your choice.

![A QR code in Lolly Demo: the colourway dots in the tool's Design system section, and the export panel recording Lolly Demo and the Spearmint colourway](/info/shots/export-panel.svg)

::: figure lolly-demo-in-use-colourways
Six colourways, each a lead, the colour on the lead and a partner, for light and dark. Choose a card to try that colourway in a link card.
:::

## Clinic: do and don't

::: figure lolly-demo-in-use-clinic
Clinic keeps the craft: a loud focus ring, a full wash for the chosen card, dashes only where you can drop, and a word with every signal.
:::

![Three theme cards in Lolly Demo, Light, Dark and Brand, with Interface follows the design system switched on](/info/shots/pd-theme-picker.svg)

## Switch to your own

::: figure lolly-demo-in-use-swap
Load your own design system and your colours, type and logos take the room. Clinic, Etiquette, Fauna and the register stay with Lolly.
:::
