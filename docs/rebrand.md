# Rebrand a deck

Lolly Rebrand reads every object in a slide deck and proposes each slide again on your design system's layouts, colours and typeface. Nothing is final until you decide: every change is a suggestion you can see, compare and answer before anything is written. Every change is a suggestion, and nothing is written until you decide.

Lolly Rebrand works on your device. The deck is not uploaded and the original file is never changed.

<!--i:eti-blend-->
## Two ways to rebrand

Choose one of two modes before you open a deck.

| Mode | What it does | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Renovate the layout** | Rebuilds each slide on your design system's slide master: its layouts, colours, typeface and marks. | The deck should look like it was made in your design system from the start. |
| **Keep the design** | Keeps every slide where it is and swaps in your theme, colours and fonts. | The layout is fine and only the look needs to change. |

The review below is for Renovate the layout; [Keep the design](#keep-the-design) has its own section.

**Renovate a deck** in four steps:

::: steps
- <!--i:eti-upload--> **[Open a deck](#open-your-own-deck)** A `.pptx`, PDF or Photoshop file, or the sample deck.
- <!--i:eti-checklist--> **[Work through the review list](#work-through-the-review-list)** One card for each kind of object across the deck.
- <!--i:eti-circle-check--> **[Decide and choose layouts](#decide-on-an-object)** Keep, replace or remove each object, and pick a layout for each slide.
- <!--i:eti-pen-tool--> **[Finish in Lolly Design](#finish-in-lolly-design)** One artboard per slide, ready to edit and export.
:::

## Open your own deck

There are three ways in:

- Choose **Choose a deck** on the Lolly Rebrand screen and pick a `.pptx`, PDF or Photoshop (`.psd`) file. You can pick several at once; they are read one after another. A Photoshop document is read as one slide: type layers become text, shapes stay shapes and other layers become pictures.
- Drop a deck anywhere in Lolly and choose the **Rebrand** view from the choices that appear.
- Open a `.lolly` file that holds a Lolly Rebrand project, and choose **Open in Lolly Rebrand**.

While the deck is read, a line says what is happening, for example "Reading slide 12 of 30", and the slides fill in underneath.

If your design system has no slide master, the Lolly Rebrand screen adds "with the neutral slide master" and the deck goes onto a plain master. A design system gets its masters, logos, colours and fonts in [Lolly Design System Setup](/info/brand-studio.html).

## The review screen

When the deck is read, the review screen opens.

![The review screen with the sample deck: the review list on the left, the proposed slide in the middle with the original above it, the decision panel on the right and the slides along the bottom.](/t/url-shot?url=%2F%23%2Frebrand%3Fsample%3Dharbourside-night-market&width=1440&height=900&dpi=96&waitMs=600&format=svg&walker=1&rasterDpi=96&filename=rebrand-review&try=1&waitSelector=.rb%5Bdata-drawn%5D&css=.float-cluster%7Bdisplay%3Anone%21important%7D)

It has five parts:

1. **The review list**, on the left: the things to check, one card each.
2. **The slide**, in the middle: the **Proposed** slide, large, with the **Original** small above it.
3. **The decision panel**, on the right: what you can do with the selection.
4. **The filmstrip**, along the bottom: every slide in the deck, in order.
5. **The footer**: how many slides and cards are left, the **Report**, **Accept all suggestions** and **Open in Design**.

### Suggestions and decisions

The Proposed slide shows every suggestion applied. A suggestion is not a decision. Nothing leaves Lolly Rebrand until you answer each card, or choose **Accept all suggestions** to take them all as shown.

Every change can be undone with **Undo**, next to the counts in the footer and in the top bar.

## Work through the review list

![The review list for the sample deck, grouped into Needs attention, Suggestions and Layouts.](/t/url-shot?url=%2F%23%2Frebrand%3Fsample%3Dharbourside-night-market&width=1440&height=900&dpi=96&waitMs=600&format=svg&walker=1&rasterDpi=96&filename=rebrand-queue&try=1&waitSelector=.rb%5Bdata-drawn%5D&cropSelector=.rb-queue&css=.float-cluster%7Bdisplay%3Anone%21important%7D&drive=click%3A.rb-review-toggle)

A card stands for one kind of object across the deck, such as "Replace marks on 8 slides", so you answer the old logo once. The list has three groups:

- **Needs attention**: something Lolly Rebrand is unsure of or that needs your choice, such as what replaces the old logo.
- **Suggestions**: what Lolly Rebrand proposes for everything else, such as removing the old page decoration.
- **Layouts**: the layout Lolly Rebrand suggests for slides whose shape it recognised. **Match** applies them all at once.

Each card is in one of three states: **Needs attention**, **Not reviewed** or **Accepted**. The tabs at the top switch between **To review**, **All slides** and **Removed**, which lists everything taken out so you can bring an object back.

## Compare the original and the proposed slide

![The sample's second slide shown Both ways: the original deck's slide beside the proposed one in the design system.](/t/url-shot?url=%2F%23%2Frebrand%3Fsample%3Dharbourside-night-market&width=1440&height=640&dpi=96&waitMs=600&format=svg&walker=1&rasterDpi=96&filename=rebrand-compare&try=1&waitSelector=.rb%5Bdata-drawn%5D&drive=click%3A.rb-cmp-seg%20%5Bdata-side%3D%22both%22%5D%3Bwait%3A1200&cropSelector=.rb-compare&css=.float-cluster%7Bdisplay%3Anone%21important%7D)

Choose **Original**, **Proposed** or **Both** above the slide. To see the original without changing the view, hold the slide or the backslash key (\\).

To decide on an object, click the object on the slide. The caption under the slide gives the layout in use and points out anything to check, such as text that no longer fits its box.

## Decide on an object

![The decision panel for the old logo: Keep, Replace or Remove, and whether to apply the choice to all eight marks or only this one.](/t/url-shot?url=%2F%23%2Frebrand%3Fsample%3Dharbourside-night-market&width=1440&height=900&dpi=96&waitMs=600&format=svg&walker=1&rasterDpi=96&filename=rebrand-decide&try=1&waitSelector=.rb%5Bdata-drawn%5D&cropSelector=.rb-decide&css=.float-cluster%7Bdisplay%3Anone%21important%7D)

The decision panel's heading says what is selected, such as "Mark on slide 2" or "Slide 3 of 9". Under **Decision**:

- **Keep**, **Replace** or **Remove** the object.
- **Replace** offers what to put in its place: **Use the design system mark**, **Choose a picture** or **Add a labelled stand-in**, and for a photo **Treat with Lolly Filter** or **Treat with Lolly Darkroom**. An endorsed or co-branded deck that must keep a second mark can answer **Keep** for that card; see [Brand architectures](/info/brand-architectures.html).
- **Apply to** chooses between every object of this kind in the deck and only this one. Objects you have changed by hand keep your change unless you tick the box that includes them.

When Lolly Rebrand is unsure of text it read, a **Text** field shows the reading for you to correct.

Further down the panel:

- **Objects** lists everything on the slide and what will happen to each one.
- **Colours** shows each colour the deck used, grouped by what it does (backgrounds, text, accents, chart series), and the design system colour it becomes. Change any of them, lock one or choose **Shuffle** for another arrangement.
- **Fonts** shows which typeface replaces each of the deck's.
- **Background** sets this slide, or the selected slides, to Light, Dark or Brand colour.

## Choose a layout for a slide

![The layout chooser for the sample's third slide, with its three cards: the layouts that fit it first, then every layout by kind.](/t/url-shot?url=%2F%23%2Frebrand%3Fsample%3Dharbourside-night-market&width=1440&height=900&dpi=96&waitMs=600&format=svg&walker=1&rasterDpi=96&filename=rebrand-layouts&try=1&waitSelector=.rb%5Bdata-drawn%5D&drive=click%3A.rb-thumb%3Anth-of-type%283%29%7Cat%3D0.5%2C0.3%3Bwait%3A900%3Bclick%3A.rb-decide%20%5Bdata-act%3D%22change-layout%22%5D%3Bwait%3A1200&cropSelector=.rb-lc-pop&css=.float-cluster%7Bdisplay%3Anone%21important%7D.arch-groups%7Bbackground%3Anone%21important%7D)

Choose **Change layout** in the decision panel, or the layout name on a slide in the filmstrip. The chooser shows:

- **This slide**: the current layout, the one Lolly Rebrand suggests, others that also fit, **Original arrangement** (everything stays where it was, in the design system's colours and typeface) and **Keep as a picture** (the slide stays exactly as it was, as one picture).
- Every other layout, grouped as **Titles**, **Text**, **Boxes**, **Pictures**, **Data** and **Steps and lists**.
- **Search layouts**: type what you want, such as "4 boxes" or "two content".

Point at a layout to preview the layout on the slide. Click to apply. A switch counts the other slides of the same shape, for example **Also the 2 slides like this one**, and applies your choice to them too. **Match** proposes a layout for every slide Lolly Rebrand recognised in one step and never changes a layout you chose.

A slide with more than its new layout holds continues onto another slide. The caption then reads "Part 1 of 2", and the report lists what moved.

## Pick a deck theme

![The deck theme choices, each drawn with the sample's first slide: Light and Dark, then the design system's looks.](/t/url-shot?url=%2F%23%2Frebrand%3Fsample%3Dharbourside-night-market&width=1440&height=900&dpi=96&waitMs=600&format=svg&walker=1&rasterDpi=96&filename=rebrand-theme&try=1&waitSelector=.rb%5Bdata-drawn%5D&drive=click%3A.rb-theme-btn%3Bwait%3A2500&cropSelector=.rb-theme-pop&css=.float-cluster%7Bdisplay%3Anone%21important%7D)

The theme button in the top bar shows the deck's colours. Open it to choose **Light** or **Dark** for the whole deck, **Brand colour** where the design system has one, or one of its saved **Looks**. A managed design system, one its catalogue locks, offers no Looks here. Each choice is drawn with your own first slide. If a theme would make some text hard to read, the choice warns you first. **Background** in the decision panel changes a single slide.

## Arrange the slides

![The filmstrip of the sample deck: nine slides in order, the second one selected.](/t/url-shot?url=%2F%23%2Frebrand%3Fsample%3Dharbourside-night-market&width=1440&height=900&dpi=96&waitMs=600&format=svg&walker=1&rasterDpi=96&filename=rebrand-filmstrip&try=1&waitSelector=.rb%5Bdata-drawn%5D&cropSelector=.rb-strip&css=.float-cluster%7Bdisplay%3Anone%21important%7D)

The filmstrip is the deck in order. Drag a slide to a new place. Its menu (the three dots, or Shift+F10) offers **Change layout**, **Leave out** or **Include**, **Move earlier**, **Move later**, **Move to position**, **Reset slide** and **Open in Design**. **Select several** changes or moves many slides together.

A slide you leave out is not deleted: it stays in the project, and **Include** brings it back.

Press **?** to see every keyboard shortcut. The common ones:

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| Arrow keys, K and J | Previous and next slide |
| Shift and an arrow | Select more slides |
| Alt and an arrow | Move the slide one place |
| L | Change the layout |
| \\ | Hold to show the Original |
| Ctrl or Cmd and Z | Undo |
| Esc | Back to one slide |

## Finish in Lolly Design

![The Lolly Rebrand report for the sample deck: what was removed, what was placed, and the counts.](/t/url-shot?url=%2F%23%2Frebrand%3Fsample%3Dharbourside-night-market&width=1440&height=900&dpi=96&waitMs=600&format=svg&walker=1&rasterDpi=96&filename=rebrand-report&try=1&waitSelector=.rb%5Bdata-drawn%5D&drive=click%3A.rb-foot-report%3Bwait%3A1000&cropSelector=.rb-report-sheet&css=.float-cluster%7Bdisplay%3Anone%21important%7D)

When the review list is empty, **Open in Design** becomes the main button. If cards are still waiting, Lolly Rebrand first asks whether to accept them as proposed. **Accept all suggestions** does the same in one step, and **Undo** reverses that.

**Open in Design** opens the result as a new Lolly Design document with one artboard per slide. Edit it there and export PowerPoint, PDF or pictures. Opening the result again later makes a new revision; the first one is never overwritten.

![The sample deck opened in Lolly Design, each slide an artboard on the design system's slide master.](/t/url-shot?url=%2F%23%2Frebrand%3Fsample%3Dharbourside-night-market&width=1440&height=900&dpi=96&waitMs=600&format=svg&walker=1&chrome=1&rasterDpi=96&filename=rebrand-in-design&waitSelector=.rb%5Bdata-drawn%5D&drive=click%3A.rb-foot-open%3Bwait%3A900%3Bclick%3A.modal%20%5Bdata-act%3D%22ok%22%5D%3Bwait%3A9000%3Bclick%3Atext%3DLanterns%20on%20the%20pier%3Bwait%3A600%3Bpress%3A%3D%3Bwait%3A300%3Bpress%3A%3D%3Bwait%3A300%3Bpress%3A%3D%3Bwait%3A300%3Bpress%3A%3D%3Bwait%3A600%3Bpress%3AEscape%3Bwait%3A1500&cropSelector=.tool-stage&css=.float-cluster%7Bdisplay%3Anone%21important%7D.fc-ruler%2C.fc-ruler-corner%7Bdisplay%3Anone%21important%7D%5Bdata-box-id%5E%3D%22r.ppt-slides-slide1.xml%22%5D%2C%5Bdata-box-id%5E%3D%22r.ppt-slides-slide2.xml%22%5D%2C%5Bdata-box-id%5E%3D%22r.ppt-slides-slide8.xml%22%5D%2C%5Bdata-box-id%5E%3D%22r.ppt-slides-slide9.xml%22%5D%7Bdisplay%3Anone%21important%7D)

The **Report**, in the footer, accounts for every object in the original deck: the objects removed, anything not placed or not drawn, the layouts used and the counts for the rest. Anything Lolly Rebrand could not place on a slide is listed under **Not placed** and waits on its own artboard in Lolly Design.

## Keep the design

![Keep the design with the sample deck: the result large, the original inset, and Download .pptx beside it.](/t/url-shot?url=%2F%23%2Frebrand%3Fmode%3Dkeep%26sample%3Dharbourside-night-market&width=1440&height=900&dpi=96&waitMs=600&format=svg&walker=1&rasterDpi=96&filename=rebrand-keep&try=1&waitSelector=.rb-keep-layout%5Bdata-status%3D%22ready%22%5D&css=.float-cluster%7Bdisplay%3Anone%21important%7D)

Keep the design replaces the former **Theme swap** tool. Choose **Keep the design** on the Lolly Rebrand screen or in the top bar. Lolly Rebrand swaps the theme, colours and fonts and says what changed, for example "Changed 5 theme colours, 4 slide colours and 2 fonts". Compare the result with **Original**, **Result**, **Both** or **Wipe**, then choose **Download .pptx** or **Open the result in Lolly Design**.

::: note Keep the design takes PowerPoint files only
Open a PDF or a Photoshop document with Renovate the layout instead.
:::

## Decks made of pictures

Some decks are pictures of slides, such as a scanned PDF or a PowerPoint file with one picture per slide. Lolly Rebrand says so ("All 15 slides are pictures") and offers **Read the text**, which rebuilds each slide from the text on its picture, or **Keep as pictures**.

Reading the text uses a text recognition model on your device. The first time, Lolly Rebrand offers to download the model and gives its size. The download sends nothing from your deck. The review list then holds a card such as "Rebuilt 15 slides from pictures", marked "Check each before Lolly Design".

<!--i:eti-folder-plus-->
## Your projects

Every deck you open becomes a project, saved on this device. **Recent projects** on the Lolly Rebrand screen reopens one where you left it, with every decision you made. To move a project to another device, choose **Download .lolly** from the project menu (the three dots in the top bar) and open that file there.

## From the command line

The same stages run from the command line and from AI agents: `lolly rebrand plan`, `compile` and `inspect`. [Rebuild a deck from the terminal](/info/cli-decks.html#renovate-in-place-with-lolly-rebrand) covers them.

::: details Words you will see
| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| Suggestion | What Lolly Rebrand would do with an object. Shown on the Proposed slide, but not final until accepted. |
| Card | One entry in the review list. A card can stand for the same kind of object on many slides. |
| Needs attention | Lolly Rebrand is unsure, or needs your choice before it can go on. |
| Slide master | The set of layouts, colours and furniture a design system gives its slides. |
| Original arrangement | The slide's objects stay where they were, in the design system's colours and typeface. |
| Keep as a picture | The slide stays exactly as it was, as one picture. |
| Left out | A slide that will not go to Lolly Design. It is not deleted, and Include brings it back. |
| Not placed | Content that fits no layout. It goes to its own artboard in Lolly Design. |
:::

<!--i:eti-info-->
## Good to know

Lolly Rebrand's suggestions, layout matches and recognised text are its best reading of the deck, not a guarantee. Check them in the review; the report shows every outcome. Export a Keynote or Google Slides deck as PowerPoint first. Layer effects and adjustment layers in a Photoshop document are not carried, and the report lists each one.
