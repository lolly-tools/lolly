# Lolly Chart

<a id="chart"></a>

Paste a table and get a chart in your design system's colours and fonts: a vector chart for a report, a statistical plot for an analysis, a lit 3-D scene for a launch slide or a cinematic flight for a video. **Lolly Chart** draws one table four ways, starts from 43 templates and exports to 20 formats, from SVG and PowerPoint to MP4.

**A chart**, in four steps:

::: steps
- <!--i:eti-table--> **[Paste your data](#make-a-chart)** Cells from a spreadsheet, or CSV.
- <!--i:eti-sparkles--> **[Pick a goal or a template](#start-from-a-template)** Chart suggests a form, or you choose one.
- <!--i:eti-palette--> **[Style the chart](#colour-and-style)** Palette, style and, for 3-D, camera and finish.
- <!--i:eti-download--> **[Export](#export-and-share)** A still, a slide, a print file or a video.
:::

## Make a chart

1. [Open Lolly Chart](/#/tool/chart) and choose a template, or **Let Chart recommend**.
2. Copy cells from Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers and paste them into **Data**. They arrive as a clean table. You can also type or paste CSV or TSV. The first row holds the column headers, the first text column becomes the labels and every numeric column becomes a series.
3. Under **Change chart**, choose **What to show**: compare values, show change over time, show parts of a whole, understand a distribution, explain a relationship, show range or uncertainty, or tell a cinematic story. Chart reads the table, explains what it assumed, maps the columns and switches to a form that suits the goal. **Choose the form myself** keeps every choice manual.
4. Choose a **Render**: Vector, Stats, 3-D or Cinema. Then pick the **Chart type**, **Statistical form**, **3-D chart type** or **Cinematic form**.
5. When the table has more columns than the chart needs, **Field mapping** sets which column is the category (X), which are values (Y), and for some forms which column sets depth, size or the animation frame.
6. Add a **Title** and a **Subtitle / source**, then export.

## Colour and style

Every colour comes from your design system. **Palette** chooses how: the design system's colours in order, colour roles first, the categorical set, a sequential or second-hue ramp, a warm or cool tint, a diverging ramp, monochrome, or a ramp from one base colour. **Colour by** gives each series, each category or each value its own colour; by value, the colourway's ramp runs from low to high.

**Style** sets the character of the whole chart: **Design system** (the default), **Editorial** (quiet and direct), **Technical** (precise and dense), **Poster** (bold and minimal), **Isometric** (vector depth), **Studio 3-D** (design system materials) and **Glass 3-D** (translucent). A **Transparent background** leaves the chart ready to place on a slide or a photo.

## The 3-D stage

The 3-D renderer builds a real scene: bars by category and series, series as solid walls, a heat map raised into tiles, one block for each unit, a running-total waterfall, a city of buildings sized like a treemap, donuts and pies, rose wedges, funnels and pyramids, packed bubbles, a coiled spiral, an x, y and z scatter, and a value surface.

- **Camera** picks the view: **Hero** (a three-quarter view that fits the artboard), **Low angle**, **High overview**, **Straight on**, **From the side**, **Top down**, **Isometric** or a custom angle.
- **On the canvas**, drag to turn the chart, Shift-drag to pan and scroll to zoom. The arrow keys turn the chart while the canvas has focus, and a double-click returns to the chosen camera. **Adjust view** keeps the result as numbers you can type, saved as an adjustment to the chosen camera; choosing another camera starts again from zero.
- **Lens** sets the perspective: standard, wide, long or flat.
- **Stage** sets what the chart stands on: a studio in one colour, a floor with a grid, no floor, a plinth or a night backdrop.
- **Finish** sets the material: flat colour with exact swatches, matte, satin, gloss, metal, clay, glass, glow, or your own roughness and metal.
- **Framing** fills the frame, closes in on the focus or leaves room to breathe.
- **Scene motion** adds a loop for video: reveal the data, rise one by one, orbit the camera, sweep across and back, or reveal and orbit.

## Cinematic flights

Cinema moves a camera through the data, so a chart becomes a short film for a launch, a keynote or a social post:

- **Spotlight tour** stops on the values that stand out.
- **Countdown** ranks down to number one.
- **Pull-back reveal** opens on one value, then shows them all.
- **Gate tunnel** flies through the values.
- **Terrain flight** passes over peaks and low ground.
- **Data flight** follows the values.
- **Ribbon canyon** compares series as walls of a canyon.
- **Constellation** tours the observations.
- **Data skyline** flies past the values.

![The finished pose of a spotlight tour: the regions as 3-D bars, each in its own colour](/t/url-shot?url=%2F%23%2Ftool%2Fchart%3Ftemplate%3Dspotlight-tour%26full&width=1440&height=900&dpi=96&waitMs=9000&walker=1&format=svg&cropSelector=%23tool-canvas&rasterDpi=192&filename=chart-tpl-spotlight&try=1)

Export a flight as MP4, WebM, GIF, animated WebP or APNG. A still export takes the finished pose, so a PNG or a slide shows the whole chart, never the empty first frame.

## Charts people can read

Every chart carries a written description and a table of its values. The description is part of the chart, so a screen reader reads out the title and how many rows and series the chart holds, and the accessible table gives the numbers. **Colour by** and **Palette** let you choose design system colours that read apart.

## Export and share

Chart exports to PNG, SVG, PDF, a print-ready CMYK PDF or TIFF, PowerPoint, Penpot, EMF, WebP, JPG and AVIF, and as motion to MP4, WebM, GIF, animated WebP and APNG. **CSV**, **JSON** and **Markdown** export the data. Vector exports keep the text as outlines, so the fonts travel with the file. [Exporting](/info/exporting.html) covers sizes, print and video settings.

Every setting fits in a link. This one opens two months of web and store orders as 3-D bars, ready to edit or export ([open the example](/#/tool/chart?rm=scene&sct=bar3d&t=Orders%20by%20channel&d=Month,Web,Store%0AJan,42,30%0AFeb,48,31)):

```text
/#/tool/chart?rm=scene&sct=bar3d&t=Orders%20by%20channel&d=Month,Web,Store%0AJan,42,30%0AFeb,48,31
```

`rm` picks the renderer (`vector`, `statistical`, `scene` or `cinematic`), `ct`, `pf`, `sct` and `cft` pick the form, `t` is the title and `d` the table. [URL Mode](/info/url-mode.html) lists every key, and the same keys work as `--name=value` flags on the [command line](/info/cli.html).
