# Lolly for sales teams

You sell. The deck, the one-pager, the case study and the leave-behind are how the story travels, and the story changes faster than any design queue can follow. Lolly turns whatever device you carry into the studio that keeps up.

## Walk in with exactly what you need

- **Wait on nothing.** On the road, the meeting is tomorrow and the file does not exist yet. Pick a tool, fill in a few fields and the finished file is ready in seconds - start with [Make something in 60 seconds](/info/make-something.html).
- **Fix the deck you already have.** Drop the PowerPoint on Lolly and its slides open straight away. Keep the ones worth keeping and reuse them as sharp vector art, then rebuild the deck from a few lines of Markdown and send back a native, editable `.pptx`. The round trip is in [Exporting & Formats](/info/exporting.html).
- **One person, the whole campaign.** Every rep, in every region, works from the same rules and gets the same finish. Nothing looks rushed, even though it was made in seconds, and nothing is a one-off.
- **The work is done before they ask.** A tool built the first time somebody needed a file makes it again in seconds, at any hour, in any time zone.

## With the rest of the team

A colleague can join you on the same design live - two people, two devices, one file, no server in the middle. See [Working together](/info/collaborate.html).

Day-to-day habits that pay off - saved sessions, projects, share links and the batch grid - are in [Using Lolly](/info/using.html).
