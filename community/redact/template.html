What will not be removed

  • A bar destroys only the pixels it covers. Invisible watermarks such as Google's SynthID span the whole image and this tool cannot detect or remove them.
  • A bar's width can still hint at the length of what it hides. Rounding widths up to a coarse grid softens this, it does not remove it.
  • Scans of printed pages can carry tracking dots from colour laser printers. The scanned page mode drops the yellow channel they live in, other print forensics remain.
  • There is no blur or pixelate option on purpose. Blurred text has been recovered in practice, a solid fill has no inverse.
